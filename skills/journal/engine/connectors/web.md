# Web and news

- **Access:** the agent's web search and fetch tools.
- **What's new:** items published since `last_run` on the run's topics.
- **Keep:** nothing by default; a one-line summary with a link in the run's output. Save a `note` only when the person asks.
- **Limits:** at most a few items per run; say why each matters to the person's goals or work.
