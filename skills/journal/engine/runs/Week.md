# Week

Consolidate the week, then review it. Fridays at noon, as the week wraps up.

## Lens
- **This quarter's goals:** progress on each.
- **Wins, what slipped and why, what was noise.**
- **Decisions this week.**
- **Habits:** streaks and misses.
- **New scope?** Only with evidence (AGENTS.md, section 3).
- **Loose ends** to close before the weekend (max 3), **next week's focus** (max 3).
- **Reflection:** 1–2 questions.
